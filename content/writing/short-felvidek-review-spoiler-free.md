Yes, **it's good!**

Oftentimes, as I get older, I get the feeling that "they just don't make them like they used to" in regards to new games that come out. The style, the writing, the "current-thing-ification" of the media — even the "good" titles just lack the magic of the games released in the 90s and early 2000s, the era I personally consider the "golden age" of gaming.

**Felvidek** is genuinely one of those few games that feel truly timeless. It's witty, it's well written, it's sincere and it respects your intelligence. Just go in blind; don't look up reviews, guides, don't even look at the discussions online so as to not spoil yourself anything. The game is pretty short (it took me 8 hours to beat), but nonetheless those 8 hours are well paced and packed with good storytelling and emotional moments. You might miss some side quests on your first playthrough, so there is _some_ replay value I suppose.

Also, I must give praise to the soundtrack of the game; it's unique and memorable and it fits the game surprisingly well. I enjoy listening to some of the tracks while coding, or drawing. My favorite tracks are "Ahriman" and "Čertova Čaša".

Pure kino.

P.S. "You acquired a Jew" — truly, a sentence I never expected to see in a game. And you actually get a Jew in your inventory! It makes sense in context, I promise.
